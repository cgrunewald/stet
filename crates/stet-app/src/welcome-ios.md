# Welcome to Stet

A quiet place to write Markdown. This note is yours to edit or delete.

## Getting around

- **Tap** the text to put the cursor there and bring up the keyboard. **done**, at the bottom right, puts the keyboard away.
- **menu**, beside it, lists everything Stet can do. Type to filter.
- **Drag** to scroll. **Pinch** to make the text larger or smaller.
- **Tap twice** to select a word, three times for a paragraph. Keep the second tap down and drag to select more.
- **Touch and hold** for the menu of what is under your finger: cut, copy and paste, or accept and reject on a suggestion.

## Your documents

Stet keeps documents in its own folder, which the Files app shows as **Stet** under *On My iPhone* or *On My iPad*. Put Markdown files there and they appear in the file browser: **menu**, then *Show or hide the file browser*.

Changes to a document are saved when you leave the app. A new page is saved as `Untitled.md` when you close it; *Go to file…* in the menu finds it again.

## Review

Suggestions are written in the text, so they survive any other app:

Stet is {~~an okay~>a quiet~~} place to {++really ++}write.

Touch and hold one to accept or reject it.

## With a keyboard

Attach a keyboard and the shortcuts in the menu work as they do on a Mac. Vim keys are there for the asking: **menu**, type `:vim`.
